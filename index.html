Departments

Five departments, one family of doctors

From a fever to a pregnancy to keyhole surgery, your care stays with the same hospital and the same records.

  • General Medicine Diabetes, thyroid, blood pressure, fever, breathing and kidney problems, and general consultations. Explore the department
  • Obstetrics & Gynaecology Pregnancy care with electronic fetal monitoring, and women's health including PCOD. Explore the department
  • Ultrasound Scanning Pregnancy, abdominal and pelvic scans, read by the doctor treating you. Explore the department
  • Infertility Treatment Tests and treatment for couples who are finding it hard to conceive. Explore the department
  • Laparoscopic Surgery Keyhole operations through small cuts, for a shorter stay and quicker recovery. Explore the department

How we treat you

Before any treatment begins

  1. 1

    We explain what we find

    After the examination and any tests, your doctor tells you what they found, in plain words and in your language.

  2. 2

    We show you the options

    Medicines, a procedure, or watching and waiting: you hear what each choice involves before deciding.

  3. 3

    We agree a plan with you

    Treatment begins only when you and your family are comfortable with the plan.

About the hospital

A family hospital for Tanuku

General physicians and gynaecologists work under one roof, so a mother-to-be, a parent with diabetes and a young couple hoping for a child can all be seen by the right doctor, close to home. Scans, reports and follow-ups stay together at one hospital, from the first consultation to discharge.
