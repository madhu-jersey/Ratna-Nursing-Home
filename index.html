Why choose

What families in and around Tanuku can count on, every time they walk in.

  • 24-hour emergency care

    The emergency department is open day and night, every day of the year, including Sundays.

  • Experienced specialists

    Two general physicians and two gynaecologists, including a gold-medal OB-GYN surgeon, see every patient in person.

  • Mother and baby care

How we treat you

Before any treatment begins

  1. 1

    We explain what we find

    After the examination and any tests, your doctor tells you what they found, in plain words and in your language.

  2. 2

    We show you the options

    Medicines, a procedure, or watching and waiting: you hear what each choice involves before deciding.

  3. 3

    We agree a plan with you

    Treatment begins only when you and your family are comfortable with the plan.
